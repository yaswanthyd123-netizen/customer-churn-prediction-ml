Responsible Data Card — Customer Churn Training Data

Dataset identity

12 rows, 7 columns, no missing values found. File: customer-churn-training.csv.

Provenance and consent

Source, collection method, consent/lawful basis, and licensing are not documented in the file. Confirm these before real-world use.

Fields

  • customer_id — identifier; exclude from features
  • tenure_months — tenure in months
  • support_tickets — ticket count
  • monthly_spend_inr — monthly spend
  • last_login_days — days since last login
  • plan_type — Basic, Standard, or Pro
  • churned — target label (0/1; definition and timeframe need confirmation)

Intended use

Educational offline model demonstration only. Not suitable for production or automated customer treatment.

Limitations and risks

  • Only 12 records; evaluation is unstable.
  • No timestamp or churn prediction horizon documented.
  • Feature definitions and label quality are not independently verified.
  • Potential privacy, leakage, false-positive, false-negative, and fairness risks.
  • No subgroup data available to evaluate fairness.

Safeguards

Use human review; define error costs; monitor data quality and drift; permit abstention; document rollback to manual process; restrict access and define retention.

Required follow-up

Confirm provenance, lawful basis, data dictionary, feature timing, label window, sampling, and retention before any operational use.
